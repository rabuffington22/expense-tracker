# Operations Monitoring And Currentness Contract

Status: canonical Phase 6 Task 1 monitoring contract completed locally through 6A, Task 2 recovery-currency contract aligned through 6B, Task 3 maintenance-review contract accepted locally through 6C-R, and Task 4 triage, retained-risk, and phase-exit governance accepted locally through 6D; every mutable external and protected surface remained unqueried

## Contract Rules

- **Source-verified** means current tracked source defines the behavior.
- **Locally verified** means a bounded synthetic or static check ran in the stated evidence block.
- **Historical** means a dated sanitized record established a fact then, not now.
- **Externally unverified-current** means the mutable external surface was not queried in the stated block.
- **Protected** means observation requires credentials, real data, provider settings, or another closed surface.
- Cadences below are the intended currentness contract. They do not prove that an observer or automation exists.
- Record observations in Central Time and include the exact target, timestamp, evidence label, result, and next authorization gate.
- `monitored` means a known risk remains visible under a defined signal or promotion trigger. `due` means an intended review or observation window arrived without passing current evidence. `decision-needed` means credible evidence requires Ryan's disposition. `waiting-on-Ryan` means the next observation or action is protected or otherwise requires Ryan. `planned`, `parked`, `stopped`, and `resolved` retain the exact meanings in the 6D governance contract.
- A signal, stale result, failure, or alert never authorizes diagnosis, retry, repair, dispatch, sync, deployment, database action, or another remediation.

## Observation Record Minimum

Every external, hosted, or protected currentness record must state:

1. the exact target and evidence label;
2. Central Time observation time;
3. the authorized request, query, or inspection count;
4. the source SHA, PR, workflow run, deployment, or provider scope when applicable;
5. the sanitized result and threshold comparison;
6. what the observation does not prove;
7. the escalation state and smallest next authorization gate;
8. confirmation that no remediation followed unless separately authorized.

- Integrity verification is intended weekly on separately authorized disposable copies and becomes stale after eight days.
- An isolated disposable-`DATA_DIR` restore drill is intended once per calendar quarter and becomes stale when the prior quarter has no passing drill or the latest pass is older than 100 days.
- Recovery currency is reviewed monthly, stale after 35 days, and reviewed again after a migration, storage/topology change, backup-method change, incident, or suspected loss/corruption.
- The target recovery point objective is no more than 36 hours once a mechanism is implemented. No recovery time objective is adopted until an authorized drill measures time to a ready-to-cut-over state.
- A bare `.sqlite` copy is never presumed complete while WAL state may contain committed work. Future acquisition must use a reviewed SQLite-supported online backup or a proven quiesced snapshot boundary.
- Observation, acquisition, integrity verification, restore drill, application-readiness proof, live replacement, and retention cleanup are separate authorization rungs. Passing one never authorizes the next.
